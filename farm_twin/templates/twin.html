Seasonal Yield Trajectory (day-by-day)

Real crop yield is only measured once per harvest — day-by-day or month-by-month yield records don't exist in agricultural data. What this chart honestly shows instead: how the yield forecast would shift day-to-day based on drought/heat stress accumulated so far this season. It's a live projection, not a fabricated historical record.
